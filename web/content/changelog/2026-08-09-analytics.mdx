"You spent ₹17,041 this cycle" is a fact. "Saturday is your heaviest day — a
third of everything" is something you can actually do something about.
Analytics has been rebuilt around the second kind.

Habits show up here that a monthly total hides completely. Spread across

is a habit.

### Also

- Charts respect your **reduced-motion** setting — no animation if your system
  asks for none.
- **Cycle labels use your timezone**, so a cycle that starts on the 1st no
  longer reads as the 31st.
- Analytics is now a **tabbed surface**, and the charts are lighter and quicker
  to draw.

### Fixed

- **Wrapped** now appears only in the last few days of the month, worked out in
  your own timezone — and stays away entirely when the month has nothing worth
  looking back on.
