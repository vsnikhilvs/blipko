You could always log a spend by texting. Now you can ask about one.

```
You:    am I spending more than last month?
Blipko: Yes — spending is up this cycle: ₹2,000 so far vs ₹400 for all of
        July (+₹1,600).

        ⚠️ This cycle is only day 9 of 31, so that's a partial month against
        a full one.

You:    can I afford a 5000 phone?
Blipko: Yes ✅
        Wants: ₹13,800 left → ₹8,800 after
        ₹383/day for the remaining 23 days

You:    set aside 2000 for the Goa trip
Blipko: Ready to move ₹2,000 into your Goa trip box.

        ➡️ Add ₹2,000 to Goa trip
        [ ✅ Confirm ]  [ ✖️ Cancel ]
```

### The numbers aren't guessed

This is the part that matters. Every figure comes out of your own records, and
every comparison is worked out before the AI is involved — affordability is a
yes/tight/no decision made in code, cycle comparisons arrive with the difference
already calculated. The AI chooses what to look up and how to say it. It doesn't
do the arithmetic.

Which also means it will tell you when it doesn't know. Ask about a category
with nothing logged and you get "nothing recorded" — never a confident ₹0.

### Changes are proposed, not made

It can edit a spend, delete one, set up a recurring rule, or move money into a
box — but it only ever proposes. You get a one-line summary built from the
actual record it would touch, and Confirm and Cancel buttons. Nothing is
written until you tap.

### It can explain itself

Ask what it can do, how something works, or where your dashboard is, and it
answers from a maintained list rather than improvising — so it won't invent a
feature that doesn't exist.

### Also

- **Dates work in your timezone.** "Yesterday" means your yesterday, including
  late at night, when it used to mean the server's.
- Questions about a specific category no longer come back empty when you've
  customised that category.
